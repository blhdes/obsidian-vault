# Doppio logotype — prompts

Prompts for generating a **clean, minimalistic, abstract** logotype for [[Projects/Doppio/Doppio|Doppio]]. Paste them into an image generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram…) as-is, or hand them to a designer as a brief.

## The idea bank (what the mark can stand for)

Doppio's whole identity is **"two"**:

- The **half ↔ double** BPM relationship (×½ / ×2)
- **Two concentric rings** breathing on the beat (the Pulse style)
- Two rhythms at once — one bold, one faint
- *Doppio movimento* (play twice as fast) + the **double espresso** nod
- A metronome **bent into a circle**

A good abstract mark picks **one** of these and says nothing else.

## Shared style suffix

Append this to every prompt below to keep results consistent:

> flat vector logo, minimalist, geometric, single color on plain background, no text, no gradients, no 3D, generous negative space, suitable as an iOS app icon

## Prompt 1 — Two concentric rings

> Abstract logo mark: two concentric circles, the inner ring bold and the outer ring thin and faint, suggesting two rhythms pulsing at once

*Closest to what the app actually shows on screen.*

## Prompt 2 — The ×2 / ×½ ratio

> Abstract geometric logo: one full circle beside a half circle, same stroke weight, perfectly aligned on a shared baseline, expressing a 2:1 ratio

*Reads as "double and half" without any numbers.*

## Prompt 3 — Doubled letterform

> Minimal logotype mark: a lowercase letter "d" duplicated and offset, the second copy half the opacity, overlapping to form a moiré-like double shape

*The "doppio = double" pun built into the letter itself.*

## Prompt 4 — Bent metronome

> Abstract logo: a single thin arc sweeping around a small dot, like a metronome needle bent into a circle, frozen mid-swing

*Echoes the Orbit/Sweep beat styles.*

## Prompt 5 — Espresso nod

> Minimal abstract mark: two small identical circles side by side inside a larger circle, evoking a double espresso seen from above, strictly geometric

*The subtle coffee reference — keep it abstract, no cup or steam.*

## Wordmark (the text part)

> Clean geometric sans-serif wordmark for the word "doppio", all lowercase, even letter spacing, the two letters "p" mirrored or subtly doubled, black on white

## Notes for picking a winner

- Must survive at **app-icon size** (~60 px) — if a detail vanishes when small, cut it.
- One color first. The app's 12 themes supply color later; the mark itself should work in plain black.
- Test it on both a **dark and light** background (the app lives in both worlds).

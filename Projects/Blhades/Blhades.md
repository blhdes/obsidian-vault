# BLH*DES

Project index for the **BLH*DES** fashion brand — an avant-garde luxury streetwear label.

## What it is
A monochrome (black/white only) collection of heavyweight boxy t-shirts, treated as art objects: limited to an **Edition of 50**, **€55**, organic combed cotton (240 gsm), cut in Portugal. The current drop is the **"White Canvas" collection** — 9 pieces (I–IX) fusing religious iconography, gothic/blackletter type, devotional text, and dark editorial photography.

## The 9 pieces (White Canvas collection)
- **I — Eternal Abode** (Bhagavad Gita manifesto, back text)
- **II — Liberated From** (collar-wrap text)
- **III — Annunciation** (Byzantine icon + Cyrillic, black/white variants)
- **IV — No Rules** (chromatic-blur type)
- **V — Repent** (gothic blackletter, double-print)
- **VI — White Canvas** (black-on-black tonal)
- **VII — The World Is On Fire** (devotional text, black/white variants)
- **VIII — Inspiration Proves** (motion-blur type)
- **IX — ✻** (houndstooth-filled star glyph)

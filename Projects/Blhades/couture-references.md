# Couture & Luxury-House References

> Analysis of the BLH*DES "White Canvas" collection (9 pieces) and which couture/luxury houses its visual language draws on. We'll go deeper on any of these later, piece-by-piece.

## First, an honest framing

Strictly speaking, **none of this is haute couture** — that's a legally protected Paris term for hand-sewn, made-to-measure one-off garments. What's been built is **avant-garde luxury streetwear**: blank heavyweight boxy tees (drop shoulder, mock neck, cropped boxy fit), monochrome only, limited to 50, treated as art objects. So the right comparison isn't "which couture house makes this t-shirt" — it's "**which houses invented the visual language being borrowed.**" And on that front the references are very specific and very legible.

## The threads, and who owns each one

**1. Religious icon prints on black (strongest, most "couture" thread)**
*ANNUNCIATION* (Byzantine icon + Cyrillic "БЛАГОВЕЩЕНИЕ"), the Caravaggio *Taking of Christ* hero image.
→ This is pure **Riccardo Tisci-era Givenchy (2010–2017)** — he built an entire empire fusing Catholic Madonnas and Byzantine icons onto black jersey. Behind him: **Jean Paul Gaultier's** Orthodox-icon couture (the Russian/"Les Vierges" themes, which is exactly why the Cyrillic works), **Dolce & Gabbana's** FW2013 Byzantine mosaic collection, and **Gianni Versace's** legendary 1991 Byzantine collection. BLH*DES took Versace's *gold* maximalism and stripped it to austere black/white — closer to Givenchy's restraint.

**2. Gothic blackletter + long doom-text down the back**
*REPENT REPENT*, *all glory be to thee O'Lord*, the Bhagavad Gita manifesto (*ETERNAL ABODE*).
→ This is the **Vetements / Demna-at-Balenciaga** signature almost exactly — biblical/heavy-metal blackletter, deadpan scripture in plain Helvetica, text wrapping the whole back. Sibling: **Enfants Riches Déprimés** (luxury-punk nihilist slogans).

**3. Devotional minimalism + the blank "canvas" concept**
*WHITE CANVAS* (black-on-black tonal), *THE WORLD IS ON FIRE*.
→ **Yeezy / Sunday Service-era Kanye** (scripture, heavyweight blanks, reverent monochrome) crossed with **Maison Margiela's** conceptual blank-canvas/anonymity tradition. The black-on-black tonal print is a Margiela move.

**4. The covered-face, monastic, cult photography**
Masked model (*WORLD ON FIRE*), white-out eyes/black lips, desert-at-night flash shots.
→ **Rick Owens** ("goth ninja," monastic darkness) and again **Margiela**, who literally covers every model's face. This is what pushes BLH*DES from "streetwear brand" toward "fashion house."

**5. Glitch / motion-blur type and the ✻ glyph**
*INSPIRATION PROVES* (blurred), *NO RULES* (chromatic-blur), the houndstooth-filled star.
→ The blur is contemporary digital/anxiety typography (think **Balenciaga's** distorted logos, **Heron Preston / 1017 ALYX** energy). Quiet detail worth noticing: filling the ✻ glyph with **houndstooth** is a direct **Dior/Chanel** tweed wink — the one explicitly "old-couture" gesture in the whole drop.

## The one-line verdict

If the collection collapsed into a single sentence: **"Givenchy-by-Tisci religious iconography, dressed in Vetements/Balenciaga gothic text, shot like Rick Owens and Margiela, with a Yeezy devotional tone."**

- Closest *living* sibling brand: **Enfants Riches Déprimés** (luxury price, punk-religious nihilism, art-object scarcity).
- Closest *historical* couture anchor: **Tisci's Givenchy**.

# Site structure — one-page scroll

Decided 2026-06-12. One single page; the menu doesn't open other pages, it scrolls you to each section. Order matters: strongest proof first, contact last.

## 1. Hero (the first screen)

What a visitor sees before scrolling. Three jobs: who you are, what you do, why keep scrolling.

- Name + a one-line identity (e.g. *"creative & app maker"* — exact wording comes from the bio work).
- 1–2 sentences max. No paragraphs here.
- One visual anchor: a photo, or an arrangement of work peeking from below.
- A "see my work ↓" cue.

## 2. Apps

The strongest section — real shipped products. Leads.

- One card per app: name, one-sentence description, 2–3 screenshots, **App Store link** (the link is the proof).
- The 2 published apps now; Doppio / Warket / Village get added as they ship.
- Optional small line under each: what *you* did (design, code, idea) — recruiters ask this.

## 3. Photography

- Curated grid, **10–20 best shots only**. A tight selection of 12 looks better than 40 mixed ones.
- Click to view large (lightbox).
- If the photos split into clear groups (e.g. events vs. portraits), 2–3 subtle category labels — otherwise one clean grid.

## 4. Projects

The "range" section — shows you're more than apps.

- Cinema background work (selected, not everything).
- **Fashion brand: reserved spot.** Placeholder until launch (~Sep 2026), then it becomes a centerpiece with brand identity + product photos.
- Each project: title, year, one paragraph, 1–3 images.

## 5. About

The story, not a CV.

- Short bio in English: *cinema → apps → brand* (drafted in the bio step — see [[Projects/Portfolio/bio|bio]] when it exists).
- One good photo of you. Optional: 3–4 plain-word skill tags.
- One honest line about what you're looking for: *open to creative roles in Europe.*

## 6. Contact (footer)

- Email (the main ask), App Store developer page, Instagram/relevant socials.
- No contact form — a `mailto:` link is enough and never breaks.

## Cross-cutting decisions

- **Language:** English only for now.
- **Mobile-first:** recruiters open links on phones.
- **Design tone:** decided later, after the bio — the site's look should match the story it tells.
- **Hosting:** free (GitHub Pages or similar); domain decision later.

# 🗂️ Sources & Directories to Mine

The databases, directories and job boards we crawl to fill the [[Projects/Portfolio/Job-Search/Job-Search|city tables]]. Grouped by type. Most let you **filter by city** — use that to feed each city note.

## Cross-industry platforms (best starting points)
- **Welcome to the Jungle** — https://www.welcometothejungle.com/en/companies — 3,500+ companies with rich profiles (photos/video), strong in Paris + EU tech/creative. Filter by city + sector. *Top pick.*
- **The Dots** — https://the-dots.com — creative people + companies network (UK/EU).
- **Creativepool** — https://creativepool.com — creative-sector company rankings + jobs.

## Creative / design agency directories
- **Sortlist** — https://www.sortlist.com — 100k+ agencies, filter by city (e.g. `/s/creative/barcelona-es`). EU-focused.
- **Clutch** — https://clutch.co — agency directory + reviews, filter by city.
- **DesignRush** — https://www.designrush.com — ranked agencies by country.
- **Dexigner directory** — https://www.dexigner.com/directory — design studios by location.

## Design-studio job boards (curated, smaller, higher-fit)
- **If You Could** (official It's Nice That jobs) — https://www.ifyoucouldjobs.com — design/creative roles, well curated.
- **The Brand Identity – Jobs** — https://the-brandidentity.com/jobs — brand/graphic/motion studios; London, Berlin, Barcelona, remote.
- **Design Week jobs** — https://www.designweek.co.uk/jobs/
- **Creative Lives in Progress** — https://www.creativelivesinprogress.com/jobs — strong for junior/entry creative roles.
- **Design Jobs Board** — https://www.designjobsboard.com

## Fashion
- **FashionUnited – Company Directory** — https://fashionunited.com/companies — top fashion companies per country + jobs.
- **BoF Careers (Business of Fashion)** — https://www.businessoffashion.com/careers/jobs/europe/ — creative + business roles across Europe.

## Music industry
- **Music Business Worldwide – Jobs** — https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/jobs/location/europe/
- **Resident Advisor** (electronic/dance; London + Berlin HQ) — roles on their site + via MBW.
- **Doors Open** — https://www.doorsopen.co — music-industry jobs by city.
- **Music Ally jobs** — https://musically.com/jobs/

## Advertising
- **AdForum agency search** — https://www.adforum.com/agency — 24k+ agencies, filter by city (Paris, London, Amsterdam, Berlin).
- **D&AD** + **LIONS / Cannes Lions** award lists — a curated who's-who of top creative shops to mine for names.

## Scenography / experiential / spatial design
- **Dezeen Jobs** — https://www.dezeen.com/jobs/ — design / architecture / exhibition / spatial roles.
- **Frame** — https://frameweb.com — spatial & experiential design studios.
- *No single clean directory — mine Dezeen Jobs + Frame + agency sites for "experiential / spatial / set design" studios.*

## Tech / startups (product + brand roles)
- **Welcome to the Jungle** (again — strongest for EU tech).
- **Wellfound** (ex-AngelList) — https://wellfound.com — startup jobs.
- **Local startup maps** — Berlin Startup Map, StartupAmsterdam, Station F (Paris) directory.

## City-specific job boards (great for entry/junior, English-friendly)
- **Berlin** — Creative City Berlin https://www.creative-city-berlin.de/en/jobs/all/ · Berlin Design Network https://berlin-design-network.org/jobs · iAgora (English speakers) https://www.iagora.com/work/en · startup lists: Failory / Seedtable / Wellfound (Berlin)
- **Amsterdam** — StartupAmsterdam https://www.startupamsterdam.org · I amsterdam business https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/business · Welcome to the Jungle (NL) · Glassdoor "creative Amsterdam"
- *(Add equivalents for the other cities as we fetch them.)*

## How to use this list
1. Pick **one source + one city** per session.
2. Pull companies that fit the hybrid profile → drop into the matching city note as `todo`.
3. Don't judge yet — just gather. Filtering is Phase 2.

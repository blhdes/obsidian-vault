# 🧭 Job Search & Relocation

The plan to land a **creative job** — first locally, then abroad. Companion to [[Projects/Portfolio/Portfolio|Portfolio]] — the portfolio is the *thing we show*; this note is *who we show it to, and how*.

> **Strategy pivot 2026-07-11 — local first.** Moving abroad stays the mid-/long-term goal, but the nearer, easier win is a better-paying **local** job (Barcelona area, home base Sant Cugat del Vallès; Madrid as option 2) that funds the future move. Focus: creative agencies, production houses, marketing — companies where the fit is real and being happy there is plausible, not just any job.

## The target

**Entry point: an assistant / junior / auxiliary role** — not senior. Somewhere to get in the door and grow.

**Areas of interest, by current fit:**
1. **Fashion & arts** — brands, studios, galleries, ateliers.
2. **Design / creative studios & agencies** — brand, content, digital craft.
3. **Tech / product** — *strongest hands-on experience*: shipped iOS apps (Swift), front- & back-end, AI / agentic engineering. Favour design-led / creative product companies and the **fashion- / culture-tech crossover** (where tech meets the creative interests).
4. **Photography / visual** — the photography + cinema eye.

**Music = future interest, not a current hook.** Only ~1–2 months of DJ/producing; we want to grow into it later, so keep music companies *secondary / aspirational*, never the headline pitch.

Honest assets for an assistant role: real **photography** experience, a trained **visual eye** (cinema), **organised project execution**, **app/product** building, languages (ES/CA/EN). Not the film industry.

## Where (priority order)

**Stage 1 — local, now (pivot 2026-07-11):**

| Rank | City | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barcelona / Sant Cugat & around** | Home turf — commutable from Sant Cugat, no relocation cost, strong production + agency + studio scene |
| 2 | **Madrid** | Spain's biggest ad/production market — but means moving within Spain (housing conditions apply) |

**Stage 1 conditions:** salary clearly above the current ~€1,000/mo · creative work, not just any job · a place where the fit is real and we could be *happy*. Everything saved goes toward Stage 2.

**Stage 2 — abroad, mid-/long-term (unchanged ranking):**

| Rank | City | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paris** | Big creative + tech scene, music/fashion crossover |
| 2 | **Amsterdam** | Strong design studios, English-friendly workplaces |
| 3 | **Berlin** | Music-tech capital (Ableton, NI), creative, livable |
| 4 | **London** | Largest creative/music market (mind post-Brexit visa) |

**Wildcards on the table (added 2026-06-14, not yet fetched):** **Mexico City** (native-language + cinema soul — the bold far-leap), **Milan** (fashion capital + Mediterranean warmth), **Malta** (English-official, EU, sunny tech hub). *(Berlin also reads as the best day-to-day personality fit.)*

**Conditions that make the Stage-2 move worth it:**
- **Salary** clearly above the current ~€1,000/mo.
- **Housing** affordable **alone** — room for a **home studio**: books, computers, screens, DJ decks.
- **English-speaking** workplace.

## The plan — slow and steady

Every phase is small. We never do it all at once.

**Phase 0 — Setup** ✅ *(2026-06-14)*
Vault structure, criteria, sources list — done.

**Phase 1 — Fetch (research)** ← *current*
Claude mines the directories and fills the city tables with candidates, **one city or one industry per session** (no overwhelm). Everything starts as `todo`.

**Phase 2 — You review & filter**
You read the candidates at your own pace and mark `★ shortlist` or `✗ pass`. Gut feeling is enough.

**Phase 3 — Deepen the shortlist**
For each `★`: find the right contact (person + email), note their recent work and the exact angle for the pitch.

**Phase 4 — Contact** 🔒 *gated*
Starts **only once the portfolio is a shareable v1** (needs real screenshots + photos + portrait — see [[Projects/Portfolio/Portfolio|Portfolio]] roadmap). Then: a short, personal email + a tailored letter per shortlisted company. Reuse the [[Projects/Portfolio/Job-Search/application-templates|base application texts (EN)]] — just swap the `[brackets]`. Track replies here.

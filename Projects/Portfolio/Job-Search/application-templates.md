# ✉️ Base Application Texts (EN)

Reusable English candidacy text for job platforms ([[Projects/Portfolio/Job-Search/sources|Welcome to the Jungle, Otta, Wellfound, The Dots…]]). Two versions: a **short** one for quick-apply message fields, and a **cover-letter** one for formal applications. Only the `[brackets]` change per send.

**Strategy:** lean on the real angle — unusual path (cinema → photojournalism) + apps shipped solo to the App Store — instead of faking seniority. For junior/entry roles that initiative is the selling point.

---

## Short version (quick apply / recruiter message)

> Hi [name / team],
>
> I'm Ale Gómez, an entry-level [product designer / developer] with an unusual path: I came from filmmaking and photojournalism, then taught myself to build software with AI. In the last few months I've shipped two iOS apps to the App Store on my own — design, Swift, front- and back-end — with more in the pipeline.
>
> [One line, specific to them: what about this company/role made you apply.]
>
> I'm based in Barcelona and ready to relocate to [city]. My work lives at **alegomez.studio**.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> Ale

---

## Cover-letter version

> Dear [hiring manager / team],
>
> I'm writing about the [role] position at [Company]. I'm an entry-level [designer / developer] with a background that doesn't read like a typical CV — and that's the point.
>
> I trained in filmmaking and worked as a photojournalist before discovering what I really love: building products with AI. Over the last few months I taught myself to ship — and I did. **Culla** and **CullaMusic** are live on the App Store, built solo end to end: interface design, Swift, front- and back-end, the lot. Three more apps are in progress, and I'm launching my first fashion brand later this year.
>
> What I bring isn't ten years of experience — it's the ability to take an idea and turn it into something real, with a designer's eye and zero hand-holding. [One or two lines on why *this* company: their product, their mission, something specific you admire.]
>
> I'm based in Barcelona and ready to relocate to [city] for the right team. You can see everything at **alegomez.studio**.
>
> I'd love to talk.
>
> Best,
> Ale Gómez

---

## What to swap each time
- `[role]` / `[designer / developer]` → match the posting (product, design, dev, creative…).
- **Company-specific line** → never skip it. One honest sentence is what separates a mass-send from a reply.
- `[city]` → Paris, Amsterdam, etc.

## Salary reference (gross/year, junior tech/product profile)
- **Barcelona:** ~€28k → ~€1.850/mo net (12 pagas).
- **Paris:** ~€40k → ~€2.350/mo net (after *prélèvement à la source*). Higher cost of living, esp. rent.

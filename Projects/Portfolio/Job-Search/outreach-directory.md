# 📮 Outreach Directory — direct-contact emails

**Goal: ≥150 companies with a direct email** for Stage-1 local job inquiries (Barcelona/Sant Cugat + Madrid). **Status: ✅ 202 companies with email, fetched 2026-07-11.** System: [[Projects/Portfolio/Job-Search/Job-Search|Job-Search]].

> ⚠️ **Honest caveats:**
> - Emails are as published — a few will bounce or be stale. Normal; move on.
> - Section B is the **first ~130 (alphabetical)** of ~500 companies in the Film Commission directory — more tranches available on request.
> - Big network agencies (Ogilvy, DDB, TBWA, Publicis…) publish no emails — they hire via portals/LinkedIn, so they're not in this list.
> 🔒 **Phase-4 gate unchanged: no sending until the portfolio is a shareable v1** (screenshots + photos + portrait). This list is ammunition, not a starting gun.

## Writing decisions

- **No em dashes ("—") anywhere in site copy.** They read as AI-generated. Use periods, commas or colons; restructure the sentence if needed. (User feedback, 2026-06-13.)
- **Failed ECIB projects stay private** (user's call) — the cinema chapter is framed as *foundation*: festivals, screenwriting lab, programming. No need to show shorts to prove the background.
- **Photojournalism era (16–24) not shown** in the photo grid (weakest/oldest work, user's call) — but the *8 years of it* stays in the bio as credibility.
- **The arc**: cinema taught him to imagine films he had no resources to make → AI/agentic tools finally gave the freedom to ship ideas as real products. That contrast is the hook.
- "From Barcelona" used as shorthand (actually Sant Cugat) — clearer abroad.
- Age (28) included for now — optional, easy to cut.
- Tone: confident but factual. No "passionate about" filler.

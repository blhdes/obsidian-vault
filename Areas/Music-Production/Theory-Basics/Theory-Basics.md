# Theory Basics

Music theory learned **from zero**, only as far as a real task needs it. No textbook marathons — when making something requires understanding a concept (a scale, a key, what makes notes sound good together), we learn just that piece and write it down here in plain language.

Likely first topics:
- Notes and the keyboard layout (what the white/black keys are)
- Beats, bars and BPM (how rhythm is counted)
- Scales and keys (why some notes "belong" together) — ties into Camelot/key knowledge from [[../../Mixing-DJing/Mixing-DJing|DJing]]
- Chords (stacking notes)

Each concept gets its own short, example-driven note.

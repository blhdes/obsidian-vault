**What this session will cover (preview):**
The **"pumping"** sound — the bass volume automatically **ducks** every time the kick hits, then swells back. The defining move of dance-music low end. This is the *automatic* version of the [[kick-bass-interlock|kick/bass interlock]] we built by hand.

Likely touches: Ableton's Compressor with sidechain input, attack/release shaping the pump, how much ducking is enough.

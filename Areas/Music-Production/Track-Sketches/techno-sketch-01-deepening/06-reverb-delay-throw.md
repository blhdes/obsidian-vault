# 🌫 Deepening 06 — Reverb + Delay Throw

> Part of [[techno-sketch-01-deepening|Techno Sketch 01 — Deepening Phase]] · Step **6 of 10** · ⏳ queued — *not taught yet*

**What this session will cover (preview):**
Give the lead a **space** to sit in (reverb) and an **echo tail** on specific notes (a delay "throw") so it doesn't feel flat and in-your-face. Builds on the reverb/delay basics from [[mixing-basics-and-effects|Session 8]], now used deliberately.

Likely touches: return/send tracks vs. inserting on the channel, BPM-synced delay throws on single notes, short vs. long reverb, keeping it off the kick/bass.

# 🎛 Deepening 05 — Lead Distortion

> Part of [[techno-sketch-01-deepening|Techno Sketch 01 — Deepening Phase]] · Step **5 of 10** · ⏳ queued — *not taught yet*

**What this session will cover (preview):**
Push the acid lead through **Saturator** or **Overdrive** for grit and harmonics — the raw, analog warehouse texture. Distortion adds upper harmonics that make the lead cut through and feel "hot".

Likely touches: drive vs. output gain, why distortion = added harmonics, keeping it from getting harsh, ordering it after the filter in the device chain.

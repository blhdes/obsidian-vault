## The Detroit reference map (production terms)

What "Detroit-style" translates to at the *producing* level — the checklist this track pulls from:

| Element | Detroit trait |
|---|---|
| **Drums** | TR-909 (sometimes 808) palette; four-on-the-floor kick, clap on 2 & 4, offbeat open hats |
| **Groove** | 16th-note **swing/shuffle** on the hats — the "machine funk" feel (see [[../Techniques/swing-and-groove|Swing & Groove]]) |
| **Bass** | Syncopated and *moving* — 16th-note pushes around the kick, minor-pentatonic note choices, not just static offbeats |
| **Chords** | The signature **minor 7th/9th stab** — a short, tight chord hit (later layer; first real chord work) |
| **Pads** | Lush string-machine style pads under everything |
| **Texture** | Raw, hardware-jam feel: velocity variation, not everything grid-perfect |
| **Structure** | DJ-friendly long intro, hypnotic repetition, layers added/removed gradually |

- Bassline homework confirmed (preset is mono by default — no Voices control found on the rack, verified by ear).
- First chord work: built **Am9** (A C E G B) stepwise — triad → +7th (G) → +9th (B) — see [[../Theory-Basics/sevenths-and-ninths|Sevenths and Ninths]].
- New track with a **poly** synth (Voices ≥ 5 — opposite of the bass rule), voiced A3–B4, stabbed on weak positions:

```
         1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a
Stab   : · · ● · · · · ● · · · · · · ● ·
```

- Same swing groove on the clip. Full recipe: [[../Techniques/detroit-chord-stab|Detroit Chord Stab]].

**What's next:** the **string pad** — the same chord knowledge stretched into a long sustained bed under everything.

### 2026-07-10 — String pad

- Stab homework done ++: three A/B clips kept (full Am9 / triad-only / rootless Am9), swing applied, and a **Channel EQ** added on the stab (user's own move — formal EQ session comes in the mix phase; tip given: dip the Low band to clear the bass's lane).
- New concept: **ADSR amplitude envelopes** — see [[../Techniques/adsr-envelopes|ADSR Envelopes (Stab vs Pad)]]. Stab and pad are the same chord at opposite envelope settings.

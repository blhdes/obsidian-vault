# Loop Character Variations (Placement-Only)

A way to get several distinct-feeling loops out of one working pattern, using only **note placement and velocity** — no swing, no effects. Built on Hardgroove 134, applied to its kick+sub interlock, but the principle generalizes to any rhythmic element.

## The idea

Start from one solid base pattern. Instead of inventing unrelated variations, treat the base itself as one valid "character" (the unwavering, machine-tight version) and derive the others by making **small, targeted edits** to specific bars — not rewriting the whole pattern.

## Three reliable moves

| Move | What it does | Where to apply it |
|---|---|---|
| **Leave it alone** | Pure repetition reads as its own character — relentless, mechanical, a deliberate baseline | The whole loop, unedited — this is "loop 1," the contrast point for the others |
| **Add an anticipation note** | A single extra hit one 16th before a strong beat pushes the ear forward into that beat — especially audible if that element is sidechained, since it catches the tail of the duck and swells in | Phrase-boundary bars only (e.g. bars 4 and 8 of an 8-bar loop) — sparingly, so it reads as a lift, not a new pattern |
| **Remove one hit** | A single missing note every other bar (or every 4 bars) reads as noticeably sparser/more hypnotic, without needing to redesign anything | Alternating bars (e.g. 2/4/6/8) — resist removing more than one hit; the contrast works because it's subtle |

## Why small edits, not new patterns

Three *unrelated* patterns don't feel like the same track — they feel like three different songs. Three *variations of one pattern* (identical except for one deliberate edit) feel like the same idea breathing differently. The base pattern stays recognizable underneath every version.

## Applied on Hardgroove 134

Base: kick/sub interlock (kick on the beat, sub on the offbeat `&`, sidechained).

- **Loop 1 — Straight Roller:** the interlock, unedited, all 8 bars.
- **Loop 2 — Rolling Push:** add a sub hit on the `a` of beat 3 in bars 4 and 8 — a 16th-note anticipation into the phrase-ending kick. Confirmed: audibly swells via the existing sidechain, reads as "rolling."
- **Loop 3 — Stripped/Hypnotic:** remove the sub hit on beat 3's `&` in bars 2, 4, 6, 8 — sparser, more space, contrasts against Loop 1's density.

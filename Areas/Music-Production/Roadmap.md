Ableton Live 12 Suite — full version. Newly available:

- **Third-party VST/AU plugin support.**

Same "reach for something new when it fits" philosophy, just against a much bigger pool — not a mandate to use everything at once.

## 🛤 The Six Paths

### Path 1 — 🎼 Theory Basics
- **Status:** 🟡 Started
- **Progress:** ~40% *(keys, A minor, pentatonic/triads — [[Theory-Basics/a-minor-pentatonic|note]]; [[Techniques/syncopated-bassline|syncopation]]; [[Theory-Basics/sevenths-and-ninths|7th/9th chord extensions]]; motif writing — call & response, variation, space — [[Techniques/bleep-lead-motif|note]]; [[Theory-Basics/phrygian-mode|the Phrygian mode]] — modes beyond major/natural minor, and stacking a mode's own altered degree as a chord's color tone)*
- **Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆
- **Unlocks:** Writing real melodies; knowing why certain notes "work" together
- **Topics:** notes, scales, keys, intervals, chord triads, common chord progressions
- **First step:** [[Theory-Basics/Theory-Basics|Theory-Basics index]] → "what's a key?"

### Path 2 — 🎛 Sound Design
- **Status:** 🟡 Started
- **Progress:** ~60% *(low-pass filter, cutoff, resonance, filter envelope — [[Techniques/acid-lead-sound|note]]; [[Techniques/adsr-envelopes|ADSR amplitude envelopes]] — the stab/pad contrast; first from-scratch patch — the Drift bleep; deep from-scratch Drift synthesis — [[Techniques/drift-kick-from-scratch|kick patch]]: oscillator isolation, Pitch Mod vs Freq Mod, hardwired Amp Envelope, LFO/Drift-macro determinism, Saturator harmonic shaping, and the LFO Rate audio-rate-vs-movement threshold as a second modulation source; Simpler's own Filter (same cutoff/resonance concept, applied per-pad in a Drum Rack) on Hardgroove 134's hats, plus the "Vel-modulation needs actual velocity variation to do anything" gotcha; [[Techniques/drift-stab-from-scratch|a polyphonic Drift stab from scratch]] — Voices/poly mode, and Drift's Low Pass Type I vs Type II filter circuits (gritty vs clean); [[Techniques/auto-filter-movement|Auto Filter]] confirmed on STAB — an effects-chain filter with its own tempo-synced LFO, layered after an instrument's static filter for rhythmic movement; [[Techniques/chorus-ensemble-width|Chorus-Ensemble]] and [[Techniques/phaser-flanger-sweep|Phaser-Flanger]] introduced as further movement-effect dimensions (pitch/time thickness vs. notch/delay sweep), plus the general principle that stacking too many LFO-driven effects on one voice risks mush; [[Techniques/oscillator-unison-detune|Oscillator Unison Detune]] on the LEAD — two real detuned oscillators beating for raw/analog character, and the lesson that "too clean" is sometimes an oscillator-stage problem no amount of downstream Saturator/effects can fix)*
- **Difficulty:** ★★★☆☆
- **Unlocks:** Designing your own signature sounds instead of grabbing presets
- **Topics:** oscillators, filters, envelopes (ADSR), LFOs, subtractive vs. FM synthesis
- **First step:** ✅ Done — Hardgroove 134's KICK built from a cleared Drift patch

### Path 3 — 🎚 Deeper Mixing & First Mastering
- **Status:** 🟡 Started

## How to use this roadmap

- **Start a session** by either saying "continue the track" or naming a path ("let's do path 1", "explain compression").
- **Progress % updates** after each session that pushes a path forward (the skill keeps this in sync).
- **Difficulty is relative to a beginner** — ★ easy, ★★★★★ brain-melt.
- **No deadline, no required order.** Have fun.

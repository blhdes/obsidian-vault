# Session View Anatomy

Now that we know Session View is the **sketchbook view**, let's look at what's actually inside it.

## The five parts of the screen

1. **The Browser** (far left panel)
   - Where Ableton's instruments, drum kits, audio loops, samples and effects all live.
   - You drag things from here into the grid to use them.

2. **The Grid** (the big middle area)
   - Vertical **columns** = *tracks*
   - Horizontal **rows** = *scenes*
   - The **cells** inside = *clips*

3. **The Mixer strip** (bottom of each track column)
   - Volume slider, pan knob, mute/solo button, level meter — one per track.

4. **The Master Track** (far right column)
   - The final volume of *everything*. All tracks mix through it.
   - Master effects (compression, limiting) go here later.

5. **The Transport Bar** (very top of the screen)
   - **Play / Stop** (or `Spacebar`) — starts/stops the whole project.
   - **Tempo** (BPM, top-left) — speed of the song. Click and type to change.
   - **Metronome** icon — toggles the count-in click on/off.

## Tracks: Audio vs. MIDI

Two main types of track. The difference matters because they hold different kinds of clip:

| Type | Holds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio track** | A pre-recorded audio file | A drum-loop sample, vocals |
| **MIDI track** | Musical notes for a software instrument to play | A piano pattern played by a software piano |

Why it matters:
- **MIDI** = "play these notes on that instrument" — the instrument decides the sound, you can swap it later.
- **Audio** = "this exact recording" — the sound is already baked in.

New projects start with a couple of each. The icon at the top of the column tells you which.

## Clips: the things inside the cells

A **clip** is a small piece of music living in a cell.

- *Audio clip* in an audio track → a loop or sample.
- *MIDI clip* in a MIDI track → a pattern of notes.

Each clip has a tiny **triangular play button** on the left side of the cell.
- Click it → the clip starts playing and **loops by default**.
- Click the **square stop button** at the bottom of that track's column → it stops.

## Scenes: a row across all tracks

Each row in the grid is a **scene**. Hit the scene's play button on the right side of the row → **every clip in that row plays at once**.

This is how you build "moments" of a song: "all the clips for the intro", "all the clips for the drop", etc.

## Your first sound (walkthrough)

1. Open Live — you'll land in Session View by default (press `Tab` if not).
2. In the **Browser** (left), open **Samples → Sounds → Drums** (Suite has tons; folder names may vary slightly by version).
3. Find any audio loop — files with a **wave-shaped icon**.
4. **Drag it onto an empty cell** in an audio track. Live auto-creates a clip.
5. Click the clip's **triangular play button** → you should hear it loop.
6. Click the **square stop button** at the bottom of that column → it stops.

Congrats — first sound made in Ableton.

> **Volume tip:** the **Master** slider on the far right controls overall output. Keep it sensible to protect your ears (and your speakers).

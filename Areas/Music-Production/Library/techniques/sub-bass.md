# Sub and bass

## Summary
A mono sine or triangle sub that interlocks with the kick. Movement comes from placement and modulation, not from notes.

## Core
| Step | Direction | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Operator sine, or Drift/Analog triangle. Mono | [start] |
| Placement | Offbeats in the gaps between kicks (classic techno interlock) | [ear] |
| Note length | Short (1/16) = tight techno. 1/8 = house. Long = pad-like wash | [start] |
| Register | Root of the key around octave 1 (e.g. A1). Lower loses definition on small speakers | [start] |
| Duck | Sidechain from KICK | [ear] |
| Low end | Utility: `Mono` switch (whole signal), or **Bass Mono** + `Bass Mono Frequency` (only below the cutoff; Audition to tune it). `Width` 0% = mono | [manual] |
| Low-end budget | Nothing else below ~100 Hz except the kick | [start] |
| Groove | Same groove file as the drums, or swung hats and bass flam | [ear] Ticket to Detroit, 2026-07-10 |

```
         1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a
Kick   : ● · · · ● · · · ● · · · ● · · ·
Sub    : · · ● · · · ● · · · ● · · · ● ·
```

## Variants
- **Sub as hook:** longer notes, slow LFO on pitch or filter, and the sub carries the section instead of a drop.
- **Rolling bass:** 16ths with velocity accents and a short filter envelope.
- **Syncopated:** keep the offbeat root, add one hit on a weak 16th (e.g. the `a` of 3) and a short turnaround at the bar end. Extra notes from the key's scale, never on the kick's quarters. Style note: Detroit/house. [ear] Ticket to Detroit, 2026-07-10
- **Placement-only loop variations** from one pattern [ear] Hardgroove 134, 2026-07-17:
  - *Straight*: unedited.
  - *Push*: add an anticipation hit one 16th before the phrase-end kick (bars 4 and 8). It swells through the sidechain.
  - *Stripped*: remove one offbeat hit on alternating bars.

## Gotchas
- Three unrelated patterns sound like three songs. One pattern with one edit each sounds like the same idea breathing. [ear]

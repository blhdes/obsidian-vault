# Metallic perc and clangs

## Summary
Inharmonic percussion from FM with non-integer ratios, very short envelopes and resonators. This is the industrial side of the kit.

## Core
| Step | Direction | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FM | Modulator into carrier, non-integer ratio (explore by ear) | [?] |
| Envelopes | Very short amp. The modulator's is even shorter, for a "tick" | [?] |
| Body | Corpus or Collision for a tuned metal/tube resonance | [?] |
| Variation | Small random pitch/velocity (Random MIDI effect or velocity edits) | [?] |

## Variants
- **Clang:** drop an octave, add Corpus and some Roar.
- **Tick:** very high ratio, near-zero decay, used as a hat.

## Gotchas
- Identical repeats sound like a machine gun. Always vary velocity.

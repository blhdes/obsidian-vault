# Commercial Cull Candidates

Releases in `~/Music/Library/` flagged against the July 2026 identity shift: raw/underground/industrial techno (Oscar Mulero, Tensal), ethically anti-commercial-EDM. Almost all of these were pulled in June 2026, **before** that shift — leftovers from the old taste, not fresh mistakes.

**The bar is ethical, not sonic:** judge the release's position in the commercial machine (major label, chart act, festival-circuit brand vs. small independent, self-released) — not whether it *sounds* like raw techno. A melodic record on a tiny indie label passes; a chart-circuit record tagged "Techno" fails.

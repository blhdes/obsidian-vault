# IDM (Intelligent Dance Music)

> New genre to explore. Note kept deliberately short — expand later.

**What it is:** Electronic music that grew out of early-90s rave/techno but pulled it *off* the dancefloor — built for headphone listening rather than DJing. Expect intricate, broken-up drum programming, melancholic or strange melodies, and a "homemade computer music" feel. The name is half-ironic (it doesn't mean other dance music is dumb); it's tied to the UK Warp Records scene.

**A few artists to start with:**
- Aphex Twin
- Autechre
- Boards of Canada
- Squarepusher
- µ-Ziq / Plaid

**Source / listening list:** [Pitchfork — The 50 Best IDM Albums of All Time](https://pitchfork.com/features/lists-and-guides/10011-the-50-best-idm-albums-of-all-time/)

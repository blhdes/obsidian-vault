# Rominimal (Romanian Minimal)

Where the [[ricardo-villalobos|Villalobos]]/Perlon sound went next. **"Rominimal"** = Romanian minimal: a regional scene out of **Bucharest** that took deep minimal and pushed it **even more stripped-back, dry, and hypnotic** — with a quirky, slightly psychedelic swing all its own. The deepest end of the rabbit hole.

**Typical BPM:** ~123–127. Feels even slower and deeper than it measures — the groove rolls rather than drives.
**Typical keys:** Sparse and modal, like minimal generally — often barely any harmony at all. Mood and rhythm over notes.
**Energy curve:** **Marathon and slow-burning.** This scene is *defined* by very long, patient, low-ego DJ sets that unfold over many hours. Almost no "drops" — just a deep groove that subtly mutates.
**Era / scene:** Emerged in **Bucharest, Romania, mid-to-late 2000s**, directly downstream of Villalobos/Perlon minimal.

---

## Who started it

- The scene's core is the trio often called its "holy trinity": **Rhadoo, Raresh, and Petre Inspirescu.**
- In ~2008 they founded **[a:rpia:r]** — the label and crew that put Romanian minimal on the world map. Browsing the [a:rpia:r] catalogue is the cleanest entry into the sound.
- A wider family of producers and labels grew around them:
  - **Praslesh** — the collaborative alias of **Praslea + Raresh** (deep, sought-after [a:rpia:r] releases).
  - **SIT** — **Cristi Cons & Vlad Caia**, who also run the **Amphia** label.
  - **Cabinet Records** — another foundational Bucharest minimal label.
  - Further names to dig: **Barac, Priku, Sublee, Dan Andrei, Cezar, Bog.**

> This is a niche, vinyl-centric scene, so detailed label/release credits float around online. Cross-check on Discogs as you dig — and lean on **DJ sets** more than albums (see below).

---

## The thing that makes it different

Regular minimal/microhouse (Perlon, Villalobos) is deep but often *warm and lush*. Rominimal strips even further:

- **Dry, woody, organic percussion** — clicks, taps, hand-percussion-like hits with very little reverb. It sounds *close* and tactile rather than spacious.
- A **quirky, "off," psychedelic swing** — grooves feel slightly playful or wonky, never straight.
- **Maximum reduction** — sometimes little more than a rolling bassline, a shaker pattern, and one odd sample, held for ages.

It's minimal taken to an almost zen extreme: tiny, dry, deep, and endlessly rolling.

---

## Sound signature / technical stuff

- **Rolling, hypnotic bassline.** Usually the lead element — a deep, repeating figure you lock onto for minutes.
- **Dry micro-percussion.** Woody clicks, soft congas, shakers, ticks. Little reverb — intimate and tactile, not cavernous (the opposite of [[dub-techno]]).
- **Sparse, weird samples.** A vocal snippet, a field recording, an odd melodic blip — used as a hook you barely notice, looped and filtered.
- **Tons of space + micro-evolution.** Like all minimal: few elements, slow change, deep groove. Rominimal just dials the "few" and "slow" even higher.
- **Soft, unobtrusive kick.** Present and steady, never the star — it serves the roll.

---

## A bit of theory

- **Reduction taken to the edge.** The discipline is "how little can carry a whole track *and* a 6-hour set?" Tension comes almost entirely from groove and patience.
- **Swing & micro-timing are everything.** With so few elements, the *feel* — the slight push/pull of the percussion off the grid — is the whole musical content.
- **The set, not the track, is the artwork.** Rominimal is built for **long-form DJ journeys**. Individual tracks are deliberately understated tools; the magic is in how a DJ layers them over hours.
- **Low ego.** Aesthetically and culturally the scene prizes deep, unflashy, hypnotic mixing over showpiece moments.

---

## DJ angle

- **Tempo zone:** ~123–127. Blends seamlessly with [[ricardo-villalobos|minimal/microhouse]], deep house, and [[dub-techno]].
- **The ultimate patient-mixing school.** This is *the* style for learning long, layered, EQ-led transitions. Tracks are loops built to be ridden together for minutes — quick cuts would miss the point entirely.
- **Loop and layer.** Lock two rolling grooves together, carve them with EQ (low end from one, dry percussion from the other), and let the blend itself become the "track."
- **Pace yourself.** The art is restraint — hold a groove longer than feels comfortable, change one element at a time, build over a whole set rather than per-track.
- **Where it plays:** deep late-night and into-the-morning sets above all. Also a beautiful, understated **opening** style.

**Transitions:**
- **From:** [[dub-techno]] or deep house → tighten into the dry rominimal roll.
- **Into:** [[ricardo-villalobos|Villalobos]]-style microhouse when you want a touch more warmth/melody.
- **Within:** mostly *stay* in it — this is a destination, not a stepping stone. Let it breathe.

## Listening list

Because this scene lives in **DJ sets and vinyl**, recordings matter as much as releases.

### Start here — sets & labels
- **[a:rpia:r] catalogue** — the label's release series is the canonical entry point.
- **Rhadoo / Raresh / Petre Inspirescu — long DJ sets** — find recorded marathon sets; *this* is where the scene reveals itself. (Rhadoo especially is more a DJ than a prolific producer.)
- **Praslesh** ([a:rpia:r]) — the deep, prized productions of Praslea + Raresh.

### Go deeper
- **SIT (Cristi Cons & Vlad Caia)** — and the **Amphia** label.
- **Cabinet Records** — browse the catalogue.
- **Petre Inspirescu** — his more melodic/album-leaning work shows the scene's artier side.
- **Barac, Priku, Sublee** — pick recent EPs to round out the map.

### The pilgrimage
- **Sunwaves (SET / Sunwaves Festival)** — the legendary multi-day festival on Romania's Black Sea coast (Mamaia). Dawn-to-day-to-dawn sets — the spiritual home of the scene, live.

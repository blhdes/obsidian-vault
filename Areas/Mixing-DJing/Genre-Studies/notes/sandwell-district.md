# Sandwell District

**collective / label** · ~2002–2013, revived 2022+ · Birmingham ↔ New York ↔ Berlin

A techno collective *and* label that grew out of Regis's Birmingham imprint **Downwards**. Core members:

- **Regis** (Karl O'Connor) — Downwards boss, Birmingham industrial lineage
- **Function** (David Sumner) — New York, deep hypnotic side
- **Silent Servant** (Juan Mendez, † 2024) — also the visual designer; the stark grey/black aesthetic is his
- **Female** (Peter Sutton) — early phase
- **Rrose** — joined the final phase (~2011), debut 12"s on the label

## The idea

Anti-star anonymity: releases came as white-ish labels credited to *Sandwell District*, not individuals — the collective as the artist. The sound: grey, loop-driven, industrial-tinged hypnotic techno. This is the bridge between 90s Birmingham (Downwards) and today's raw/hypnotic scene — exactly the lane of [[industrial-gothic-techno]].

## Key records

- **Feed-Forward** (2010) — the collective's only album of its first life; canonical modern techno record
- **End Beginnings** (2025) — the reunion album (Regis + Function), finished after Silent Servant's death

## Why it grabbed me

Imported **Regis – In a Syrian Tongue** and **Function – Aeternum (Existenz)** into the Library in the same July 2026 session, then realised they're two members of the same collective. Digging one member's catalog keeps leading to the others — the collective is the map, not the individual names.

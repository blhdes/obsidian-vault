# Blaou (Sounds)

The first **classic Berlin house/techno label** I ran into via a vinyl rip. Worth knowing as a label, not just a one-off release.

## The facts (per Discogs)

- **Type:** Berlin-based **house / techno** label (full name *Blaou Sounds*).
- **Founded:** ~**1996** — first release `blaou 001`, *Audio-Video-Disco Connexions – Der Kroatische Traum*.
- **Run by:** Dittmar Frohmann; **Mathias Schaffhäuser** is the label's central, most-prolific artist (a German house/techno producer who also ran the *Ware* label).
- **Base:** Berlin (Neukölln → Kreuzberg). Label code **LC 2119**.
- **Size:** ~**52 releases**, mostly 12" vinyl, running from 1996 through the mid/late-2000s.
- **Discogs:** [Blaou — label page](https://www.discogs.com/label/1376611-Blaou) (catalogue, releases, profile).

## Why it stuck with me

I encountered it through `blaou 036` — **The KLF vs. Ricardo Villalobos – What Time Is Love? (Veto Retro mix #1 & #2)** (2005). A bootleg/edit fusing the KLF rave anthem with a Villalobos rework — see [[ricardo-villalobos]]. So my first Blaou contact was an *outlier* bootleg, but the label itself has a real 90s house/techno catalogue behind it.

## Key artists / releases to explore

- **Mathias Schaffhäuser** — the house of the house; many Blaou EPs (`Wig Wam Now`, `Nice To Meet You`, `From 4 To 6 A.M.`).
- **Erobique** (Carsten Meyer) — `blaou 013` *Erobique Invades Space* (2000); well-known name (International Pony).
- Comps worth a look: *Club Blaou – Die Hits Der Clubs* (1997), *Blaou – The Greatest Hits* (2001).

# Deconstructed Club

> New genre to explore. Landed here via Evian Christ's *Ultra* — tagged it `Experimental, Trance`, but the wider world it belongs to is this.

**What it is:** Experimental electronic music that takes the *parts* of club music — the kick drum, the rave stab, the vocal chop, the siren, the bass drop, the build-up — and pulls them apart, then rebuilds them in jagged, collage-like shapes that deliberately **refuse to lock into a steady groove**. So it keeps the *signals* of a dancefloor (the euphoria, the tension, the "here comes the drop" feeling) while throwing away the thing that makes a track easy to actually mix: the steady, repeating 4/4 beat. It nods at styles like grime, jersey/Baltimore club, reggaeton, dancehall and techno without ever settling into any of them. Also called **"post-club"** or **"club music for not-the-club."**

**Where it came from (short version):** Early 2010s, born on the internet rather than in one city. Roots trace to late-2000s New York nightlife — the **GHE20G0TH1K** parties (started 2009 by DJ **Venus X**), where DJs like **Total Freedom** pioneered a chopped-up, mashup style of mixing. UK bass labels **Night Slugs** and **Fade to Mind** fed into it, and by mid-decade it spread through netlabels and collectives across London, Berlin, Mexico City and LA. Much of it comes from queer and marginalized scenes, and the music often carries those politics openly (race, gender, queer identity, colonialism).

**Why it matters even though it's barely DJ-able:** Most of it is arrhythmic on purpose — you won't beatmatch it in a normal set. Its value for me is as *context*: it's the experimental lab that euphoric, club-facing producers borrow from. Evian Christ's *Ultra* sits right on that edge — it has the deconstructed-club love of giant trance stabs and rave euphoria, but pulled back into something you *can* actually play. Knowing this scene explains the "Experimental" half of that genre tag.

**A few artists to start with:**
- Arca
- SOPHIE
- Lotic
- Amnesia Scanner
- M.E.S.H.
- Total Freedom (the DJ who shaped the sound)

**Labels worth knowing:** PAN, Janus (Berlin), Halcyon Veil, Fade to Mind, Tri Angle, NON Worldwide, NAAFI (Mexico City).

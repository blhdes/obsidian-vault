## Verification (2026-05-23)

**Lesson learned:** Tunebat (Spotify-derived) and `keyfinder-cli` (libkeyfinder) both produced wrong keys on real material. For Message of Love, Tunebat said B min (10A), keyfinder said G♯ min (1A), and **Engine DJ said A min (8A)** — three tools, three answers. Engine DJ is the only one that matters for actually mixing.

**New rule for this tracklist:** trust **Engine DJ first**. The database lives at `~/Music/Engine Library/Database2/m.db` — its `Track.key` integer maps to Open Key notation (`key=1` → `1m` → A min → 8A Camelot).

For unverified tracks: add them to Engine DJ, analyze, then re-run this lookup.

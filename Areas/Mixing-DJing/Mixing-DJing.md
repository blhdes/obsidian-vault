## How to use this area

- Drop new manuals/cheatsheets into `Manuals/` with a kebab-case filename.
- Each tutorial series gets one note in `Tutorials/` with timestamped takeaways.
- Track prep notes (e.g. "house warmup set, 120 BPM, 8 tracks") live in `Tracklists/`.
- Genre deep-dives or theory notes go in `Genre-Studies/`.
- When I learn something general about mixing technique that doesn't fit a folder above, drop it in the relevant subfolder's index file as a dated section, or in [[../../Inbox]] if it's truly unclear.

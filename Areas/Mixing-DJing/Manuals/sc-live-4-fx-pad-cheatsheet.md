# SC Live 4 — FX & Pad Cheatsheet

What every effect and pad mode actually does. For **Engine OS 5.0** (the OS running on the SC Live 4 itself, not the desktop app).

> **Quick myth-bust:** OS 5.0 did **not** add new effects. The big FX library (26 Main FX) arrived back in **Engine OS 3.1**. All 5.0 changed on the FX side is stretching **Reverb Rise** and **Reverb Drop** to a longer **32-beat** range. The other 5.0 headlines (RGB waveforms, hardware star ratings) are display/library features, not FX. See [[engine-dj-sc-live-4-workflow]].

---

## The three FX layers

The SC Live 4 has three separate effect systems. They run in this order (signal flows top → bottom):

1. **Sweep FX** — one knob per channel. Instant, one effect at a time. For quick, hands-on color.
2. **Touch FX** — swipe the touchscreen XY pad to morph build-ups/drops. Can be latched hands-free.
3. **Main FX (BPM FX)** — the big selectable list below. Tempo-synced, the deepest control.

> Old spec sheets say "13 BPM FX" — that's the 2022 launch number. Since OS 3.1 your unit actually has **26 Main FX** (listed below).

---

## Sweep FX (the channel knobs)

Center = off. Turn left or right from center to apply.

| FX | Left (CCW) | Right (CW) |
|---|---|---|
| **Filter** | Low-pass (cuts highs) | High-pass (cuts lows) |
| **Echo** | Shorter delay, more feedback | Longer delay, more feedback |
| **Noise** | Adds pink noise | Adds white noise |
| **Wash Out** | 1-beat echo + mutes the channel | ½-beat echo (transition) |

---

## Main FX (BPM FX) — the full 26

How to fire one: **pick FX from the list → set the beat length → set wet/dry (amount) → tap ON.**

**Delays & echoes**
- **Echo** — repeats that fade out in time with the beat. The classic trailing effect.
- **Echo Out** — past halfway on the knob it mutes the dry track and throws the tail into a fading echo. Cut-to-echo before a drop.
- **Delay** — clean single repeats, tighter than Echo.
- **Ping Pong** — stereo delay bouncing left ↔ right.
- **Hold Echo** — freezes a slice and echoes it indefinitely. Build-up / transition glue.
- **Recycler** — warm, analog-style delay with saturation.
- **Riser** — glitchy delay that rises in speed and pitch, then echoes. Instant tension.
- **Stutter Out** — stylized stutter/echo-out, 13 patterns. Transition tool.

**Reverbs**
- **Reverb** — adds room/space and a decaying tail.
- **Swell Verb** — reverb that swells in as you turn the knob (room size adjustable).
- **LFO Verb** — reverb modulated by an LFO, for a moving, wobbling space.
- **Reverb Rise** — reverb that builds upward — a riser into a drop. *Up to 32 beats (OS 5.0).*
- **Reverb Drop** — sidechained reverb that "pumps," giving a dropping feel. *Up to 32 beats (OS 5.0).*
- **Reverse Reverb** — backwards reverb that swells into the sound.

**Modulation & texture**
- **Flanger** — sweeping "jet plane" whoosh.
- **Flanger (Negative)** — phase-inverted flanger; hollow, vowel-like.
- **Phaser** — swirling sweep, subtler than flanger.
- **LFO Filter** — filter that auto-sweeps with an LFO (hands-free wobble).
- **Auto Gate** — chops the audio on/off rhythmically (trance-gate / stutter).
- **Crush** — bit-crusher; lo-fi, gritty digital distortion.

**Pitch**
- **Pitch Down** — drops pitch chromatically, in time with the beat.
- **Scale Down** — pitch drop that follows one of four musical scales.

**Tape / transition**
- **Beat Break** — chops and replays slices in musical patterns (10). Breakbeat feel.
- **Backspin** — vinyl backspin sound *without* losing your place in the track (3 variants).
- **Brake** — tape/turntable slowdown to a stop.
- **Roll** — repeats a phrase in time (phase-quantized loop roll).

## Touch FX (the touchscreen)

Swipe in any direction on the XY pad to morph an effect — instant build-ups, drops, transitions. **Latch** it to keep it running and free your hands. Sits between Sweep and Main FX in the chain.

---

## Performance pad modes (8 pads)

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hot Cue** | Jump to your saved cue points. |
| **Auto Loop** | Instant loops of a fixed beat length (¼, ½, 1, 2, 4… beats). |
| **Manual Loop** | Set your own In/Out points, then save the loop. |
| **Roll** | Momentary loop roll *while held* — the track keeps running underneath, so you snap back into place on release. |
| **Slicer** | Splits a section into 8 slices across the pads; replay them out of order. |
| **Slicer Loop** | Same as Slicer, but the section keeps looping. |

---

## When to reach for what

| Moment | Grab |
|---|---|
| Long build into a drop | **Reverb Rise** or **Riser** (16–32 beats) |
| Pump / drop feel on the kick | **Reverb Drop** |
| Hard cut, leave a tail | **Echo Out** or **Wash Out** |
| Smooth blend between tracks | Sweep **Filter** to carve space, **Echo** to bridge |
| Hypnotic movement (dub / minimal) | **LFO Filter** or **Auto Gate** |
| Drum fill / breakbeat moment | **Beat Break** or **Roll** |
| Stop the track dead | **Brake** or **Backspin** |

# Melodic / microhouse track recommendations

Lesser-known, highly melodic tracks in the vein of **Pachanga Boys – Time** and **Robert Miles – Children**. Same melodic house & techno / microhouse family — they tend to *build* slowly rather than hit one big hook.

## 1. Kollektiv Turmstrasse – Sorry I Am Late
*Melodic deep house/techno · German*
Closest emotional match — a long, patient build with a heart-tugging melodic payoff, much like *Time*.
- Discogs: https://www.discogs.com/master/844904-Kollektiv-Turmstrasse-Sry-Im-Late-EP

## 2. Robag Wruhme – Thora Vukk
*Microhouse album · Pampa Records · 2011*
The microhouse pick. Title track and *Wuppdeckmischmampf* are warm, gentle, very melodic — tiny clicks and pads instead of big drops.
- Discogs: https://www.discogs.com/master/328611-Robag-Wruhme-Thora-Vukk

## 3. Minilogue – Animals
*Hypnotic melodic minimal techno · Sweden*
A ~10-minute evolving journey — that same "ride it out" feeling as *Time*, with analogue melodies that slowly morph the whole way through.
- Discogs: https://www.discogs.com/master/33266-Minilogue-Animals

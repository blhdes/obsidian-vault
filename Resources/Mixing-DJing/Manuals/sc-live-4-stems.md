# SC Live 4 — Stems Manual

**Stems** = a track split into its 4 parts — **Vocals, Melody, Bass, Drums** — so you can mute or isolate any of them live (e.g. drop the vocal of one track over the beat of another). For **Engine DJ 5.0** / **Engine OS 5.0**.

> **The one thing to know first:** the SC Live 4 **cannot separate stems on-device.** You **pre-render** them in Engine DJ Desktop on your computer, then export. (Only the RANE SYSTEM ONE renders stems on the unit itself — that's the OS 5.0 headline feature, and your SC Live 4 doesn't have it.)
>
> Trade-off: pre-rendering costs disk space + analysis time up front, but the payoff is **high-quality stems with zero processing load while you play.** No live CPU strain, no quality compromise on the table.

---

## Step 0 — Get the license (one-time)

- Stems need a **one-time unlock license — $9.99 USD**, from **store.enginedj.com**. Not a subscription.
- Use the **same email** as your Engine DJ Profile login.
- (Prime 4+ owners get it free — but the SC Live 4 needs the paid unlock.)
- After buying, open Engine DJ Desktop and power on the unit while logged into your profile; restart if prompted to enable it.

> Want to try before committing? Compatible units ship with a few **demo tracks pre-rendered for stems** so you can test the feature first.

---

## Step 1 — Render stems in Engine DJ Desktop

Two ways:

- **Right-click** a track (or select several) → **Create Stems**.
- Or **drag** tracks/playlists into the **Stems** playlist under Collection.

Analysis progress shows at the bottom of the screen. When done, a **Stems icon** appears next to each track, and they collect in the auto-generated **Stems playlist** (without leaving their original playlists).

---

## Step 2 — Export to USB/SD

1. Plug in the drive, open **SYNC MANAGER**.
2. Select the playlists already on the drive → **EXPORT TO DRIVE**.
3. Only the **new stem data** transfers — tracks aren't duplicated.
4. **Eject** before unplugging.

> Same Sync Manager flow as the main [[engine-dj-sc-live-4-workflow|export workflow]] — stems just ride along once rendered.

---

## Step 3 — Use stems on the SC Live 4

1. Load the stem-rendered track to a deck.
2. **Double-press the Hot Cue pad mode button** → the **top four pads** light up:

| Pad | Stem |
|---|---|
| 1 | Vocals |
| 2 | Melody |
| 3 | Bass |
| 4 | Drums |

3. **Single press** a pad to toggle that stem **on/off**.
4. Quick-select buttons:
   - **Left Parameter (◄) = Acapella** — vocals only.
   - **Right Parameter (►) = Instrumental** — everything except the vocal.

---

## Mixing ideas

- **Acapella over a beat** — kill everything but Vocals on track A, lay it over the Drums/Bass of track B.
- **Cleaner blends** — mute the incoming track's Bass while mixing in, so two basslines never fight. Bring it back once the old track is out.
- **Instant breakdown** — drop the Drums + Bass mid-phrase for a quick stripped section, then slam them back.
- **Swap the top** — mute Melody on one track, borrow the Melody of another.

---

## Gotchas

- **Render on the computer, always** — the SC Live 4 won't do it live.
- **Stems roughly double a track's size on the drive** — leave extra space; use **exFAT** for big libraries.
- **Re-export after rendering** — new stems only reach the unit through Sync Manager, never by copying files.
- Mind **bass clashes** — isolating Bass is the single most useful clash-fixer in a blend.
